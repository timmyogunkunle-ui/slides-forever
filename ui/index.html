    Import songs from EasyWorship

    Songs on the church computer? EasyWorship 6, 7 and 8 keep songs in
    C:\Users\Public\Documents\Softouch\Easyworship\Default\v6.1\Databases\Data
    Copy that whole Data folder to a USB stick, plug it in here and click Browse for Songs.db. Nothing in EasyWorship is changed.
